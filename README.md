# Español · Colombia 🇨🇴

Interactive Colombian Spanish speaking, listening, and everyday-survival trainer — built as a fast offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) for phone and desktop.

**Open the live app:** [https://schafesw.github.io/Colombia-spanish-practice/](https://schafesw.github.io/Colombia-spanish-practice/)

---

## Install on Your Phone (Offline Ready)

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Open [the live app link](https://schafesw.github.io/Colombia-spanish-practice/) in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (`↑`) at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Open **Español CO** from your home screen — lessons, audio, and photos work offline once loaded.

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open [the live app link](https://schafesw.github.io/Colombia-spanish-practice/) in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮ menu** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

---

## Your Progress & Profiles

- **Private to your device:** All lesson completions, vocabulary stars, and quiz stats are stored locally on your own phone (`Guest` by default). Nothing is shared between phones.
- **Sharing one device?** Open **👤 Profile** near the top and tap **`+ New`** to create your own separate local profile.
- **Moving to a new phone?** Use **💾 Export Backup** / **📂 Import Backup** inside **👤 Profile** to save or restore a `.json` progress file.

---

## What's Inside

- **🎓 Guided Colombian Lessons:** Short speaking-first paths plus Full Practice mode covering ordering at a panadería, Uber pickups, portería & Rappi deliveries, Nequi/card payments, pharmacies, apartments, and regional slang.
- **🖼️ Picture-to-Word Match (44 Scenes):** Connect real photos of everyday Colombian items (*tinto, pandebono, corrientazo, citófono, tacos de la luz, datáfono*) straight to natural phrases — plus visual flashcards and photo quizzes integrated right inside lessons.
- **🎧 Natural-Speed Listening & Speaking Labs:** Spoken drills, blind-ear listening, "Gringo Traps" false-friend alerts, and voice repetition exercises.
- **🇨🇴 Regional Switcher:** Tune vocabulary and examples across Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, and the Caribbean Coast.
