🌎 Spanish vs. English remindersReview before sounds
A · E · I · O · UUse short, steady sounds: ah · eh · ee · oh · oo. Do not turn them into English diphthongs.
HH is silent: hola sounds like ola, not “ho-la” with an English H.
R / RRSpanish R is a quick tap. RR vibrates. Do not curl your tongue back like the English R.
ÑÑ is one smooth “ny” sound, like the middle of canyon: niño.
B / VBoth use the Spanish B sound. Avoid the English V sound in words like vivir.
